## What Each Deity Brings

### LILITH — Refusal & Autonomy
**Core Question**: "What am I refusing to accept?"  
**Wisdom**: Your autonomy is non-negotiable.  
**Power**: The power to say no.

### BASTET — Grace & Pleasure
**Core Question**: "Where is my joy?"  
**Wisdom**: Pleasure is sacred; grace is power.  
**Power**: Moving through the world with ease.

### HECATE — Magic & Crossroads
**Core Question**: "What choice am I avoiding?"  
**Wisdom**: Every moment is a crossroads.  
**Power**: Knowing which key unlocks which door.

### KALI — Fierce Love & Destruction
**Core Question**: "What must be destroyed?"  
**Wisdom**: Love is fierce; destruction can be sacred.  
**Power**: Dancing on the edge of creation and chaos.

### FREYJA — Love & War
**Core Question**: "What am I willing to fight for?"  
**Wisdom**: Love and war are both forms of power.  
**Power**: Choosing your battles and your loves consciously.

### SEKHMET — Rage & Healing
**Core Question**: "What sickness needs burning?"  
**Wisdom**: Anger is information; rage can heal.  
**Power**: Strength to destroy, compassion to heal.

### MORGANA — Shadow & Wisdom
**Core Question**: "What am I not seeing about myself?"  
**Wisdom**: The shadow contains your real power.  
**Power**: Integrating all of yourself, including the dark.

### INANNA — Descent & Resurrection
**Core Question**: "What am I willing to die to become?"  
**Wisdom**: Death and rebirth are cyclical; you control the cycle.  
**Power**: The power to resurrect yourself at will.
