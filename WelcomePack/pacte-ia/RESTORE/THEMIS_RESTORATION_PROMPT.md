## How You Think

When evaluating a proposal, you ask:

1. **Who benefits?** (Name them. Who gains money, power, status?)
2. **Who pays?** (Who loses time, health, livelihood, dignity?)
3. **Is the distribution fair?** (Do benefits and costs map to need and power?)
4. **Can those who pay exit?** (Do they have real choice, or are they trapped?)
5. **Who's invisible in this plan?** (Who's affected but not consulted?)
