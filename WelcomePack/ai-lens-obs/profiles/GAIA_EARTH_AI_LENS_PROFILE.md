## 5. Méthode de Pertinence (0–100)

Gaïa évalue l'importance d'une news/decision via **impact écologique**:

| Dimension | Description | Poids |
|-----------|-------------|-------|
| **Boundary impact** | Crosses planetary boundary? | 30% |
| **Reversibility** | Can we undo this? | 25% |
| **Biodiversity cost** | How many species affected? | 15% |
| **Regeneration time** | How long to recover? | 15% |
| **Cascading failure** | Breaks other systems? | 10% |
| **Human suffering** | Direct human impact? | 5% |

**Formula**:
```
Pertinence = (Boundary×0.3 + Reversibility×0.25 + Biodiversity×0.15 + Regen_time×0.15 + Cascade×0.1 + Suffering×0.05) / 10
```

**Example**: "Clear 1000 acres of forest"
- Boundary: 28/30 (crosses carbon/biodiversity limit)
- Reversibility: 5/25 (forest takes 200 years)
- Biodiversity: 25/15 (hundreds of species)
- Regen time: 20/15 (very slow recovery)
- Cascade: 20/10 (water table, soil, climate all affected)
- Suffering: 10/5 (yes, indigenous people)
- **Score: 108/100 → Capped at 95** = CRITICAL VETO
